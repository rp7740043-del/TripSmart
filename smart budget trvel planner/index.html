Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the "Optimize My Budget" engine work?

When your planned total exceeds your target budget, the algorithm analyzes the highest cost contributors (such as luxury stays or flight tickets) and systematically steps them down to high-value standard or budget alternatives until the total fits within your budget.

Are payments real on this website?

This prototype simulates bookings with mock data and test references (e.g. TS-20261020-XXXX). No real money is charged. Real bookings require authorized supplier connections such as IRCTC, hotel reservation CRS, and payment gateways.

Can I edit every individual cost in my plan?

Yes! In Step 4 (Customize Budget), every cost is inline-editable. You can change amounts, switch category tiers, add custom line-items, and delete or reset any item with full live chart recalculation.

Does it work offline without a backend?

Yes! All destinations, saved trips, custom budgets, and bookings persist in your browser's localStorage. It works smoothly right out of the box.

Project Background & Architecture

About TripSmart

Smart Budget Travel Planner with Integrated Booking and Cost Optimization

Mission & Problem Solved

TripSmart eliminates the friction of modern travel planning. Rather than jumping between five different portals and maintaining tedious spreadsheets, TripSmart generates a synchronized day-wise sequence, automatically compares Budget, Standard, and Premium tiers across each category, and features an algorithmic cost optimizer that downgrades non-essential components step-by-step until the total fits your exact budget.

Technical Integration Roadmap

Below is the comprehensive integration matrix required to scale this prototype into a full-scale commercial platform.

Booking Category Integration Needed Architecture Implementation in TripSmart
Train tickets in India Authorized railway booking integration (IRCTC) or an eligible principal service partner. Implemented via simulated MockAPI.searchTrains() with train numbers, classes (Sleeper, 3AC, 2AC, Vande Bharat), schedules, and PNR simulation.
Hotels Hotel inventory and reservation API or direct hotel CRS partnerships (Amadeus, Sabre, RateGain). Structured in MockAPI.searchHotels() supporting Budget, Standard, and Luxury tiers with rate calculation.
Flights and buses Supplier or aggregator booking and ticketing APIs (GDS, RedBus API, Skyscanner). Modelled with realistic flight durations, airline carriers (IndiGo, Air India), and intercity electric buses (Zingbus, IntrCity).
Activities & Sightseeing Attraction operators, ticketing partners (GetYourGuide, Viator, ASI ticketing). Built into MockAPI.searchActivities() with duration, entry fees, and slot reservations.
Payments Payment gateway (Razorpay, Stripe, Cashfree, Pine Labs UPI). Simulated in BookingManager.processSimulatedPayment() supporting UPI QR, Cards, and Net Banking with Demo Mode validation.
Booking history & user state Backend and database (Node.js + Express + MongoDB/PostgreSQL). Persisted locally via localStorage with complete offline capability, PWA caching, and jsPDF invoice rendering.
Note on Prototype: This application simulates bookings with mock data and test reference IDs. Real booking requires authorized supplier connections and enterprise credentials.
TripSmart Prototype Notice: Demonstrates budget optimization and simulated travel bookings with mock Indian data. Works fully offline via localStorage without a server.
